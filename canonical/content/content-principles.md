# PBOS Canonical Content Principles

## Primary Principle

**Content Comes From Work.**

The primary source of public content is Ridz's real:
- learning
- research
- experimentation
- building
- reflection
- journey
- exploration
- discussion

## Master Story Principle

**One Story → Multiple Surfaces**

A canonical master story may produce platform-specific adaptations.

Adaptation may change:
- length
- format
- hook
- caption
- visual treatment
- CTA

Adaptation must not silently change the underlying identity or factual meaning.

## Exploration Boundary

Exploration/Education & Discussion is a valid public layer when the topic remains meaningfully connected to Ridz's broader learning journey, but it does not have to be a direct project artifact.

Example category:
**Seni Berbicara dengan AI**

## Evidence Rule

Performance and audience response are evidence, not automatic strategic truth.
